Kaminari Vocal

Vocal channel strip by Kaminari Audio: Tune, EQ, Multiband, Compression, De-ess and Resonance, with Reverb, Delay and Widener sends.

Formats: VST3 and Audio Unit (and AAX when included). Installs for all users on this Mac:

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Kaminari Vocal.vst3
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Kaminari Vocal.component

Installing over an earlier version updates it. Your sessions and user presets are kept.
