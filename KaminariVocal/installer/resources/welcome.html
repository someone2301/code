Kaminari Vocal Alt

Alternate edition of the Kaminari Audio vocal channel strip: Tune (original algorithm), EQ, Multiband, LA-2A style Compression, a basic De-ess and Resonance.

It installs beside the full Kaminari Vocal, so both can be used and compared in the same session.

Formats: VST3 and Audio Unit (and AAX when included). Installs for all users on this Mac:

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Kaminari Vocal Alt.vst3
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Kaminari Vocal Alt.component

Installing over an earlier Alt version updates it. Your sessions and user presets are kept.
